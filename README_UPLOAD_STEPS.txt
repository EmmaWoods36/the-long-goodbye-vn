This zip contains:

- assets/
- asset_manifest.csv

Yes: update BOTH index.html and the assets folder in GitHub.

How to upload:
1. Open your GitHub repo.
2. Click Add file > Upload files.
3. Drag in index.html.
4. Drag in the entire assets folder, or open assets and upload all files into your repo's assets folder.
5. Commit changes.
6. Wait a minute or two for GitHub Pages to update.
7. Open the site in an incognito/private window to test a fresh-player experience.

Important behavior:
- Saves/unlocks are per browser through localStorage.
- Your friend will not inherit your unlocked endings unless they use your same browser/profile.
- To reset your own browser, use the Clear Browser Saves button or add ?reset=1 to the end of the URL.
